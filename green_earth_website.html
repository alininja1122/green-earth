🌱 Our Mission

Our mission is to protect the environment by helping people learn simple ways to recycle, save water, plant trees, and reduce pollution. Every small action can make a difference.

🌿 How You Can Help

♻️ Recycle

Reuse materials and sort recyclable waste where local recycling is available.

🌳 Plant Trees

Support responsible tree-planting and protect existing green spaces.

💧 Save Water

Turn off taps when you do not need water and fix leaks when possible.

🌎 Reduce Pollution

Use less single-use plastic and keep streets, rivers, and parks clean.

💡 Daily Eco Tip

Turn off lights when you leave a room.

🌍 Remember

You do not have to do everything at once. Choose one helpful habit and keep doing it.
